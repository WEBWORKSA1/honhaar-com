How to turn a video into a result

Watching feels productive, but nothing changes until you act. Use a simple rule: for every video, write one sentence on what you will do differently, and do it within 24 hours. After the revision-tips video, turn tonight’s notes into ten questions and test yourself tomorrow. After the procrastination talk, open the deadline planner and put a date on every step of your next application. After the essay video, rewrite the first paragraph of your statement using a single specific moment instead of general claims.
