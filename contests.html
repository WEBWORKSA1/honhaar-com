How judging works

  • Originality (35%) — your own idea and voice
  • Impact (25%) — does it make the reader think, feel or act?
  • Craft (25%) — structure, clarity, technique
  • Theme fit (15%) — answers this month’s prompt

Rules in brief

  • One entry per person per month. Free to enter — we will never ask you to pay.
  • Work must be your own. AI-generated entries and plagiarism are disqualified.
  • Entrants under 18 need a parent or guardian’s permission.
  • Share your work as a link (Google Drive, YouTube unlisted, GitHub, etc.) set to “anyone with the link can view”.
  • You keep copyright. You allow Honhaar to display winning entries with credit.
  • Winners are announced in the first week of the following month and contacted by email.
