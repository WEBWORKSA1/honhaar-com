    Why these tools matter

    Most funding mistakes are arithmetic mistakes. Students compare countries on tuition alone and forget that living costs in London or New York can exceed tuition; they sign education loans looking only at the EMI and miss the interest that quietly compounds during a two-year course; they convert CGPA with the wrong formula and under-report their marks. These calculators make those numbers visible in seconds so that your decisions — which country, how much to borrow, which deadlines to chase first — rest on the right figures.

    All calculations run locally in your browser. We do not store or transmit what you type into the tools. If you want a human to review your plan, use the free personal match.
