Match FAQ

Why is this free?

Honhaar is funded by advertising, sponsors, donations and — only if you opt in — introductions to education partners such as universities and lenders. Students never pay us.

Will I get spam calls?

No. Without the optional partner box ticked, only Honhaar contacts you, by email. With it ticked, only partners relevant to your stated goals may contact you, and you can opt out at any time.

Can you guarantee a scholarship?

No one honestly can. We help you find the right programmes and apply well; selection is always decided by the provider.
