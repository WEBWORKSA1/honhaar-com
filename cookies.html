Cookie Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Essential storage

We use your browser’s local storage for features you control: light/dark theme, saved scholarships, your readiness checklist and your cookie choice. This data never leaves your device.

Advertising cookies

Google and its partners use cookies to serve and measure ads, including personalised ads based on your visits to this and other sites. In regions that require consent, Google’s consent message asks you before personalised ads are used.

YouTube

Embedded videos load from youtube-nocookie.com only when you press play; YouTube may then set cookies.

Your controls

  • Manage ad personalisation at Google Ads Settings.
  • Opt out of many vendors at aboutads.info.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work.
